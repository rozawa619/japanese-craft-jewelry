Cloisonné combines a metal base with fired glassy enamel. Aichi-based Kato Shippo’s SUKE collection emphasizes transparent color, while Tamura Shippo’s selected Kaisai earrings combine a pure-silver base, silver-wire drawing and enamel.

Compare the surface, outline and fitting rather than treating all enamel jewelry as one specification. The pure-silver statement for Kaisai refers to its enamel base, not every fitting. Craft designations and artisan qualifications are not treated as certification of each individual accessory.
