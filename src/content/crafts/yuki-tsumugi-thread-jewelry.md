Yuki tsumugi is woven in northern Kanto around Yuki, Ibaraki, and Oyama, Tochigi. Ibaraki’s Tsumuring project makes accessories from kirisune, the threads remaining after woven cloth is taken off the loom. The threads’ color and texture are visible in their new use.
