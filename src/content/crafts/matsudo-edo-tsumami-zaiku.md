Chiba Prefecture profiles Ayano Fujii as a Matsudo maker of Edo tsumami kanzashi, an ornamental hairpin craft. Her team adapts those fabric-folding techniques to everyday accessories. For rikka, the maker uses pointed folds in thin habutae silk.
