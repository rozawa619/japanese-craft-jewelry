KAZARINO is a Kyoto decorative-metalwork maker’s contemporary brand. Open copper ear motifs and broad brass wrist pieces bring a different surface from textile or metal inlay.

Names of traditional patterns should not be confused with materials: the Shippo bangle is patterned brass rather than cloisonné enamel. Its uncoated finish and published allergy warning are part of the choice.
