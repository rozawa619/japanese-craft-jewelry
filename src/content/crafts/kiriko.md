Kiriko means cut glass. RINZEN builds its own kiriko designs around decorative cuts and hand polishing, bringing a craft often encountered in glassware into jewelry.

Our amber selection combines a geometric outline with a clear, radiating cut. We identify the technique as the maker and product listing do: kiriko. That description does not establish individual Edo Kiriko certification.
