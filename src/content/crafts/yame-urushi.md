Iwaya in Yame connects its accessories with skills from Yame-Fukushima Buddhist-altar lacquerwork. On the selected earrings, lacquer covers a rounded mizuki-wood base.

The visible color surface is a different choice from exposed wood grain or transparent glass. Individual accessory certification, exact weight and skin suitability are not inferred from the tradition.
