SHOWEN’s Uji-made collection introduces braided silk as a jewelry material. The selected double-wrap bracelet uses a small round odamaki ball as part of its fastening.

The braid name Edo-uchi does not relocate the Kyoto maker to Tokyo. A material claim of pure silk also does not prove every stage was hand-braided. Compare the finite size with metal and glass alternatives.
