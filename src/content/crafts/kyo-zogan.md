Nakajima Zogan’s official process introduces fine grooves cut into a metal base, inlaying metal to form a motif and finishing the surface. The selected pear-shaped pendant offers floral and geometric faces.

Compare the design through the official product page. A general account of the maker’s process is not a certification or complete component list for every accessory.
