## Carved contours under lacquer

Kamakura City describes Kamakura-bori as carving designs into wooden forms and finishing them with lacquer. Yogado documents the distinct work of the wood-forming, carving and lacquer-finishing craftspeople behind its products.

Look at where the light shifts along a petal or leaf. The shape of a camellia or maple comes from the carved surface, and the lacquer brings out the changes in contour. Compare the way a pin sits on a lapel with how a chain positions a pendant. The selected listings describe materials and measurements, and any individual craft certification is unconfirmed.
