Atelier ARCOS describes shaping glass with an oxygen burner in its Kamogawa studio. Nanao & Shuka identifies a Nara workshop and its own burner-worked pieces. HARIO Lampwork Factory has a Nihonbashi workshop and production bases in other regions; the exact workshop for the selected Little Tears earrings is not specified. These are separate makers, with their locations kept distinct.

Compare a pendant or bracelet with a small glass earring. Glass composition, shaft construction and fitting size differ by piece. This collection presents contemporary studio work without claiming individual national traditional-craft certification.
