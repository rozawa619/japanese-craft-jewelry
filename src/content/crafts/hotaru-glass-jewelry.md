Kanoa describes hotaru glass as a type of lampworked bead. A maker rounds the softened colored glass, layers on silver leaf, and covers it with a clear glass layer. The silver reflects light from within the bead. Kanoa assembles the finished earrings in Okinawa and does not identify the bead maker for this model.
