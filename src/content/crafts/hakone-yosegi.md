## Colour, arranged in wood.

Yosegi combines woods of different natural colours into geometric arrangements. Hamamatsuya explains the pattern-making process through joined wooden units. Its broader practice also distinguishes thin decorative sheets from solid muku work.

Our jewelry selection follows what the catalogs actually say. Species, finish, construction and the origin of individual items are not assumed from the craft's general history.

## Tradition and the individual object

A regional craft description does not establish that every contemporary piece carries an official designation. See each object's classification and sources. No item in this first edit is presented as individually certified or physically tested by ITO.
