## The space inside the pattern

Dahliacyan describes making a small opening in silver sheet, threading a fine saw through it and cutting each section of the motif. The workshop attached to its shop in Kamakura makes original metalwork by hand.

On a pierced earring the open sections show the background around the stone. A bracelet shows the cut sheet across the wrist, with round links that let you choose the clasp position. The maker’s current listing is the place to see the outlines of its one-off pieces.
