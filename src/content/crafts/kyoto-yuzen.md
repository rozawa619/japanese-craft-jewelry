Morphosphere’s tint collection combines Yuzen dyeing with embroidered lace. Familiar flower shapes become a way to explore the color and surface texture of dyed textiles.

The selected pansy is identified as Kyoto Yuzen dyeing in its product information. We describe that contemporary jewelry use without borrowing unverified comfort or skin-suitability claims.
