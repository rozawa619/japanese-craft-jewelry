Sangetsu Kobo makes patterned glass beads associated with Osaka’s Senshu area. Izumi Tombodama is the maker’s registered name; it is not a generic label for every glass bead.

Choose between a small ear accent and a larger bead necklace. The public Sakaihigashi shop is different from the workshop/office where products are not sold.
