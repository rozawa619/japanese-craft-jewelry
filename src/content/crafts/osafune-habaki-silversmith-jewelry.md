Bizen Osafune’s museum and workshops introduce several specialist metalworking trades. The habaki is a small historical fitting traditionally made by a shiroganeshi silversmith. For this wearable design, a silversmith at the site made a miniature pendant in pure silver with a cross-lined surface.
