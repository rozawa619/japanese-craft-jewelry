Kofu-born SIMPRICH began as a factory brand of seven jewelry companies. Its range offers an entry into Yamanashi’s jewelry making through simple forms and the appearance of their materials.

Our first two selections make a useful contrast: compact quartz-and-mother-of-pearl bubble earrings, and a milky-pink rose-quartz pendant. Both are made-to-order listings. The brand identifies a museum shop for part of its collection, though individual SKU stock must be checked. This regional category does not claim the stones were mined in Yamanashi.
