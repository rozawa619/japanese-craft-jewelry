Mitsusuke describes Higo inlay as metalwork involving gold or silver set into iron. In the selected accessory, the inlaid face is only one part of the object: base and findings have their own specifications.

We introduce the technique through a contemporary jewelry design, without treating a general craft tradition as an individual product certificate.
