Akari Aso works with bamboo from the Kunisaki Peninsula at her studio in Bungo-Takada. Her contemporary accessories build on skills associated with Beppu bamboo craft. For Chiasma, flat bamboo strips cross over and under one another to form a compact, raised square. The lacquer finish brings color and sheen to the weave.
