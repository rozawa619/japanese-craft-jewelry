In Beppu, bamboo craft ranges from vessels to pieces that can be worn. Cotake's Taoyame draws attention to the slender curve of bamboo and gives only one side a chain.

In Bungo-Takada, MIKAI BAMBOO uses interlaced red and blue-green strips in Chiasma. It is another way to wear Oita bamboo alongside the gentler curve of Cotake’s Beppu design.
