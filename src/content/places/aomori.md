In Aomori, look for depth in the lacquer as well as colour. The selected Fukushi clip-ons carry the finish onto both sides of a long triangle, so the angle at which they catch the light becomes part of the design.

For a softer finish, a kogin brooch puts warm mustard stitching against an ivory cloth. The small bean pattern is a different way to wear a Tsugaru tradition, on a collar or bag.
