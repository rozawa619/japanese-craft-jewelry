The Yame lacquer studio Iwaya uses skills from Yame-Fukushima Buddhist altar making in its ear jewelry, bold lacquer pendants and hand-carved rings. In Kitakyushu, ITOHEN sets handwoven Kokura-ori into the wings of a butterfly pendant. In Itoshima, Tabinokiseki works pale blue and pink into a glass pendant inspired by the coast at dusk.

Visit Iwaya in Yame or consult ITOHEN’s opening calendar for Tobata, Kitakyushu. Tabinokiseki’s studio shop is in Itoshima. For a specific color, size or made-to-order design, start with the individual product’s confirmed order route and ask the maker before visiting.
