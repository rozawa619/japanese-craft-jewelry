The Osaka selection combines Senshu shell-based imitation pearls with Izumi glass beads from Sangetsu Kobo. The pearl items establish the origin of the pearl component, not the entire assembly location or an identified individual workshop. Sangetsu describes its glass-bead production in the Senshu area.

The glass-bead earrings and necklace offer a patterned alternative to the round pearl forms. The maker’s current Sakaihigashi shop is separate from its non-retail workshop; featured product stock must be checked before visiting.
