The exact workshop for the selected Little Tears item is not established, so it is not described as wholly Tokyo-made.

Compare angular cut glass with small drops on a chain, and choose between earrings or a pendant. Current official ordering has been observed for the selected pieces; exact shop stock is a separate check.
