The first Kumamoto selection follows Higo inlay through Mitsusuke, a workshop with a main shop in Kumamoto City. A dark iron surface and gold linework offer a different starting point from colorful glass or porcelain.

The workshop connection is verified, while fittings and store stock are checked separately. The official shop guide is a starting point for visiting; it is not a promise that the selected pierced option will be on the counter.

The new Kumamoto Castle pendant draws on the shapes of the stone walls. See it alongside Mitsusuke’s smaller ear design to compare how gold lines look across different scales.
