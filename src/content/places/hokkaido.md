Yukie Glass creates crystal-glass accessories in Otaru. ToyToy’s official brand profile describes its designer as Hokkaido Ainu and presents contemporary original Ainu-pattern designs. These are different creative backgrounds; the selection does not treat all Hokkaido jewelry as a single tradition or certify every component’s origin.

Compare the colored Veil glass drops with Ninkari wooden earrings and the Sirokani-pe necklace. The latter has a pure-silver pendant and an SV925 chain, rather than an entirely SV1000 construction. Individual shop stock is unconfirmed; the glass atelier also has advance-reservation conditions.

Kitaichi links little glass beads inspired by the Otaru Canal. In Asahikawa, Hokkaido Mizuhiki ties a pine-knot ear cuff from strands of paper cord.
