Yukie Glass makes crystal-glass accessories one piece at a time in Otaru, Hokkaido. This selection focuses on the Veil earrings: broad drops flattened into thin surfaces to show their color.

Mint Veil combines soft green glass and mica; Rouge Veil places deep red within clear glass. Compare both the color and the listed size rather than assuming the two pieces are identical. The atelier receives visitors by confirmed advance arrangement, and city stockists have different collections. No individual store stock is promised.
