SIMPRICH is a factory brand born from seven Kofu jewelry companies. The selected pieces connect readers with this manufacturing background, without claiming that the gemstones were mined in Yamanashi.

The selection now includes layered quartz and mother-of-pearl, rose quartz, a reversible white-topaz/ruby chain ring and a silver Piaring. The Piaring price is explicitly for one ear; it is not doubled into an invented pair price. Made-to-order terms and actual store stock should be checked on the official pages.

Alongside SIMPRICH, L&Co. presents a small opal cabochon and TO LABO brings contrasting finishes to a square of rock crystal.
