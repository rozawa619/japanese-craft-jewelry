Kuji's amber is fossil resin, and much of its visual interest is inside the material. Look for the subtle stripes in the dark teardrops the local workshop has cut and polished.

The new amber ring lets you see the banding on the hand. The confirmed listing is for Japanese size 13, so check the width and size before ordering.
