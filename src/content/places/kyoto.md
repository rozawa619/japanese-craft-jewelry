Kyoto’s selection includes Morphosphere’s dyed embroidered lace, Nakajima inlay, KAZARINO decorative metalwork and SHOWEN silk braid. These are distinct making practices. The shippo pattern on KAZARINO’s brass bangle is a geometric motif, not a claim that the bangle is cloisonne enamel.

Compare a blue flower or a reversible pendant with openwork metal earrings and a silk bracelet. KAZARINO explicitly does not market the selected metal pieces as allergy compatible. SHOWEN’s silk content does not establish entirely hand-braided production.
