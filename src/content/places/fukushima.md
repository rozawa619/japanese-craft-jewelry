Fukushima’s Aizuwakamatsu is home to the Sakamoto Collection and its lacquer jewelry. The crimson Tsuya Jewel is an introduction to that work.

Follow the shine of the crimson bead if you want to see what local lacquer looks like away from the table.

For a different material in Aizuwakamatsu, look for small triangular studs made with Aizu cotton. The local craft center lists the series, and the maker has a separate online choice for the triangular pierced version.
