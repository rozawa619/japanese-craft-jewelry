Choose the fastening before you choose the pattern. A beautiful pair will not serve its purpose if it requires a piercing you do not have, uses a mechanism you do not want or leaves an important material question unanswered.

Japanese shops often separate *piasu* (ピアス) from *iyaringu* (イヤリング). In the listings researched for this site, these distinguish pierced from non-pierced earrings. The label alone does not tell you the complete construction, so our product pages also identify what remains unconfirmed.

## Compare the same questions

| Question | Pierced earrings | Non-pierced earrings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What mechanism is used? | Confirm the post or hook and the back or catch. | Confirm whether it is a clip, screw or another mechanism. |
| What touches the skin? | Ask about the post, back, hook, plating and other contact parts. | Ask about the pad, clip or screw and any coating. |
| What does the size describe? | Check the ornament separately from the full drop. | Check the ornament and the fastening's dimensions. |
| Is weight published? | Confirm whether it is per piece or per pair. | Confirm whether it is per piece or per pair. |
| Can you return it? | Read the seller's rules for this particular item. | Read the seller's rules for this particular item. |

These are questions to use while shopping, not specifications shared by all products in either category.

## Keep variants separate

The same-looking ornament can be sold with different fittings. Do not assume the price, material or availability of one variant applies to another. [Hamamatsuya's official catalog](https://www.hamamatsuya.co.jp/item/muku/accessory_2.html), for instance, lists an 18K pierced option, a surgical-stainless-steel pierced option and a separate non-pierced option. That does not establish the metal of every component or the mechanism of the non-pierced version.

Our listings preserve those distinctions. If the precise mechanism is not published, “not confirmed” is the useful answer until the seller supplies it. We do not offer a fitting conversion service, and a shop's possible customization should only be described when that shop confirms it for the item.

## A material name is a starting point

A phrase such as “stainless steel” or “18K” is not a guarantee about every part of an earring, nor a promise that it will suit every person. If material sensitivity is relevant to your decision, ask for the composition and coating of the parts that touch you. Do not substitute a wood ornament's material description for the specification of the fastening.

We have not tested these products on skin or evaluated how they feel after hours of wear. We therefore make no claims that a mechanism is painless, secure for a particular activity or suitable for an individual sensitivity.

## Send a question the seller can answer

Name the exact item or variant and ask for the missing detail: “Could you confirm the fastening type, the material of each skin-contact part, the full dimensions and the weight of one earring?” For an overseas order, add your destination country and ask whether that exact item can be shipped there.

If the answer changes which item you would choose, wait for it before ordering. A clear specification is more valuable than guessing from an attractive photograph.
