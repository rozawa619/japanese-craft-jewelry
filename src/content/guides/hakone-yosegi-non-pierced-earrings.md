You do not need to start with pierced jewelry to explore Japanese woodcraft. Hamamatsuya's catalog includes a separate non-pierced earring listing within its *muku* yosegi accessories. For readers drawn to wood patterns, it is a candidate to investigate with the maker, rather than a promise about fit or comfort.

The product card below contains the checked price, date and official source. We have not worn this item or tested its fastening. Before ordering, choose the actual pair and confirm how it attaches to the ear.

## A connection to Hakone, Kanagawa

Hamamatsuya's shop and visible factory are listed in Hatajuku, Hakone. Its [craft overview](https://www.hamamatsuya.co.jp/craft/index.html) explains how different natural wood colors can be assembled into geometric patterns. The accessory listing gives a way to explore that visual language through jewelry.

The workshop connection is verified; the exact manufacturing location of an individual pair has not been independently audited. A general explanation of a traditional craft also does not certify a particular accessory or identify all the woods used in it.

## A styling idea: a warm accent beside the face

Try thinking of the pattern as the outfit's small focal point. A plain shirt and a relaxed cardigan could give it an understated setting. For a sweeter look, a simple rounded neckline and softly tied-back hair are another possibility. Select the look you enjoy without inferring that every design has the same size or weight.

These are editorial styling ideas, not a wear test. The maker's current photographs remain the place to inspect available shapes, pattern placement and supplied fittings. Ask for another angle when the back of the ornament affects your decision.

## “Non-pierced” does not settle the mechanism

The Japanese listing calls this item *iyaringu* (イヤリング), separately from *piasu* (ピアス). We group it with non-pierced earrings. “Clip-on” is a useful shopping term, but we have not confirmed the precise mechanism, adjustment range or metal composition for the pair you would receive.

Ask whether it uses a screw, spring or another fitting, and request a side or back photograph of the actual pair. Also ask for dimensions, weight per earring and the materials of parts touching the skin. We cannot tell you that it will be painless, stay secure for every activity or suit a particular sensitivity.

## Before choosing it as a gift

A non-pierced option can be worth asking a gift recipient about, but it is still a personal fitting choice. Check the recipient's preferred fastening first. If you are ordering from abroad, ask the seller whether the item can be supplied to your country and confirm the total amount and applicable return rules.
