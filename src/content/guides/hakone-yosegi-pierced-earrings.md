Looking for Japanese wooden earrings with a connection to Hakone? Hamamatsuya's catch-back pierced-earring listing is a useful place to start. It brings a small wearable object into a wider story about patterned wood, while leaving several practical questions to settle with the maker.

This introduction is based on the workshop's published information. We have not tried on, weighed or independently tested the earrings. The product card below brings together the checked catalog price, date and official link; confirm the current offer with the seller.

## Why look at yosegi jewelry?

Yosegi draws attention to how different wood colors sit beside one another. Hamamatsuya's [craft explanation](https://www.hamamatsuya.co.jp/craft/index.html) describes geometric pattern-making with naturally colored woods, and distinguishes thin applied sheets from shaped thicker patterned material. The ear accessories appear in its *muku* catalog section. This general context does not identify the species, finish or exact construction of every earring.

There is a clear regional connection: Hamamatsuya lists its shop and visible factory in Hatajuku, Hakone, Kanagawa. We have not separately audited the production location of each item, and do not present this accessory as individually certified.

## A styling idea: let the wood be the detail

For a gentle, everyday outfit, consider a plain cream blouse, a simple knit or an open shirt collar. A quieter fabric can give a geometric wood pattern room to stand out. Tucking back one side of the hair is another way to make an ear ornament visible without adding more jewelry.

These are styling suggestions, rather than an account of wearing this pair. Choose the actual shape and pattern from the maker's current images. A styled image cannot establish the supplied design, its size against your face or its color under your own lighting.

## Catch-back and 18K are separate listings

The official page names a surgical-stainless-steel option with a catch fitting, alongside a separate 18K pierced option. The image block is labelled for the 18K earrings, so we do not treat its visible hooks or patterns as proof of the catch-back variant's exact construction.

Before ordering, request a photograph of the precise catch-back pair being offered. Ask which parts the stainless-steel wording covers, and request the full dimensions and weight per earring. A metal label alone does not establish suitability for sensitive ears.

## Buying from outside Japan

An [English shop](https://www.hamamatsuya.co.jp/shop-english/) exists, but we have not verified an international checkout for this catalog item. Ask the shop whether the exact variant can be ordered for your country, and confirm stock, tax treatment, shipping charges and applicable returns before paying the seller.
