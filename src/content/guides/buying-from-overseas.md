Start with one question: can this retailer send this exact item to your country under terms you understand? An English page, a foreign-currency display or an international audience is not enough to establish that an order can be delivered to your address.

ITO introduces accessories and links to external retailers. We do not sell products, take payment, arrange shipping or handle returns. The contract and checkout are with the retailer you choose.

## Confirm the destination for the item

Read the retailer's current shipping information and, when necessary, ask about your country and the exact product. [Hakone Maruyama's overseas-shipping page](https://www.hakonemaruyama.co.jp/oversea.htm) is evidence that the shop describes an overseas service. It is not a blanket promise from ITO that every item can reach every destination today.

For a listing without confirmed overseas terms, we say that shipping remains unconfirmed. This is different from saying the seller cannot ship abroad. If you need a proxy or forwarding service, evaluate that service's own costs, restrictions and return arrangements separately; we have not assessed one for you.

## Compare the full cost

Write down the product price in the seller's stated currency, then add confirmed delivery charges. Ask how destination taxes, duties and any handling charges are treated. Their amount and collection method depend on the destination and transaction, so consult the relevant official customs information and the retailer rather than relying on a universal estimate.

Our recorded prices are dated observations. They are not a live checkout quote, a currency conversion or a guarantee of stock. Do not assume a Japanese tax-inclusive price resolves the cost of importing an item elsewhere.

## Understand the return route before paying

Find the rules for change of mind, defects, incorrect items and damage in transit. Check whether earrings have a specific restriction, how soon you must contact the retailer, whether prior authorization is required and who pays return postage. A general domestic return policy may not answer the overseas case.

[Hakone Maruyama's shopping guide](https://www.hakonemaruyama.co.jp/html/info.html) publishes return information, but you should obtain clarification if it does not answer your destination or product question. We do not extend or replace a retailer's policy.

## Check the details that cannot be fixed at checkout

Confirm pierced or non-pierced fittings, measurements, the materials of skin-contact parts and any pattern variation before ordering. If a gift must arrive by a particular date, ask about dispatch as well as transit; neither a product's availability nor a general delivery estimate is a personal arrival guarantee.

Keep the written answers, order confirmation and current policy details for your records. Avoid sending payment details or private order information to ITO; questions about an order belong with the retailer.

## A concise message to the shop

You can adapt this checklist into your own inquiry: identify the product and variant, give the destination country, request the shipping method and total delivery charge, ask who handles import charges, and confirm the return process. Include any specification the product page leaves unclear.

If a missing answer would change your decision, resolve it first. Discovering an appealing craft object and completing a well-informed purchase are separate steps.
