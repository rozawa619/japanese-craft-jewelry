In yosegi, the contrasting colors of the woods become the design itself. That makes this an appealing route into jewelry for someone whose interest is arrangement and pattern. We selected Hamamatsuya as a way to bring its workshop’s woodcraft into a jewelry collection.

There are two useful ways to narrow the search: the pattern across the wood, and the outline around it. Maruyama’s C and D designs make the outline a clear choice. Hamamatsuya’s catalog is a starting point for choosing among the workshop’s currently shown patterns.
