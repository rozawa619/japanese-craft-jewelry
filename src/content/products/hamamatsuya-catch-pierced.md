Wood colors arranged into geometry: that is the appeal we wanted to bring into an everyday jewelry collection. Hamamatsuya's catch-back listing offers a way to enjoy yosegi as something to wear, with the craft itself at the heart of the choice.

Choose it for an interest in materials and pattern, and for the connection to a Hakone workshop. Ask the maker to show the actual catch-back pair, then explore the pattern you would like to wear.
