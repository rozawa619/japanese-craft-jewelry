This is the thread-focused alternative to CO.COON’s fabric-shaped ori. Silk is worked into individual frames, so the outline and the lines running through it become the focus. A hairstyle that leaves the ear visible helps show that difference.

The official gallery credits the pierced Frame Series to hanaroi. Its craft connection comes through CO.COON’s use of silk from Hakata-ori artist Kiyo Araki. The gallery does not publish a fixed price, measurements or fitting alloy. Check the current design and sales guidance directly rather than assuming every pictured version is available.
