Design D gives yosegi the role of a narrow line. Beside the wider patterned surface of a pendant or the radiating shape of C, its long outline becomes the most useful distinction. The fine wood pattern remains, but the composition is more vertical.

Treat the wood color and the straight outline as two separate choices. Together they make a route into craft jewelry for a tailored wardrobe. Compare the outline with C before choosing which role you want the earrings to play.
