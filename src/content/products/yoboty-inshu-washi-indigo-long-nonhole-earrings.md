The shape is almost a drawn line, but the surface belongs to paper: pale fibers remain beside areas of stronger indigo. Rolled washi puts a familiar Japanese material into a vertical outline without turning it into a flower or a knot.

It is a choice for texture as much as color. A plain shirt leaves the narrow form readable; a wide patterned collar may make it less distinct. The current selection is specifically the non-hole variation, which should be checked separately from the pierced version on the official page.
