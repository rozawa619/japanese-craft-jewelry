A rice grain is a modest subject for jewelry, which is exactly why it makes an interesting souvenir. It connects the ornament with Akita without relying on a place name written across the design. In silver filigree, the subject is carried by fine line rather than a broad field of color.

Choose this direction when you want a small detail to discover at close range. A quiet shirt or knit gives the silver room to read clearly. Compare it with the floral CHECOS design or the abstract SIRI SIRI hoop to decide whether a recognizable local motif is the part you most want to keep.
