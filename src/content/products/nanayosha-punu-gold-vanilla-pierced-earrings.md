Punu brings some of the pleasure of choosing pottery into a small piece of jewelry. The vanilla color is quiet, but the shape has depth; the gold decoration adds a separate glint rather than replacing the ceramic character. It is a useful alternative when a long earring feels like more line than you want.

Try it beside a navy collar or a plain knit and let the rounded form carry the detail. Compared with the half-moon and chain lines of 6.KILN or an enamelled metal drop, the decision here is about ceramic volume, a warm pale color and the small contrast of gold.
