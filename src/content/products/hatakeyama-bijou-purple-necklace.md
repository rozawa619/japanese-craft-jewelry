A necklace can add color without becoming the largest part of an outfit. Here the small floral geometry is the reason to choose it: purple sits inside a warm gold-tone outline, with open space around the pattern.

Compare the compact shape with a larger RINZEN leaf pendant if you want more visible glass, or with ToyToy’s silver pendant if you prefer an all-metal effect. The chain length and the way the motif sits are worth checking in the official photographs.
