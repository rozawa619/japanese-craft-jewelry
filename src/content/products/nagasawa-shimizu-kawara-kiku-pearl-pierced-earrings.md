The flower gives these earrings their outline; the pearl changes the mood. At the ear, the dark circular motif reads as a small sculpted form, with a pale drop moving below it. An open neckline gives the pair room, while a plain shirt makes the two contrasting surfaces easy to see. The clay’s rougher appearance is part of the appeal here, alongside the pearl’s lustre.
