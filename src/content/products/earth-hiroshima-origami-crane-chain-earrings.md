The appeal of this crane is its small scale against a long line. A familiar origami subject becomes a silver detail that can be noticed as the chain moves, leaving the rest of the outfit quiet. It offers a different souvenir direction from a large emblem or a brightly coloured ornament.

Keep the neckline simple and think about the space around the ear: hair, a scarf and a raised collar all change how a long chain will sit. Compare it with RINZEN’s amber glass chain for a warmer colour, or a compact SIRI SIRI hoop when you want silver to stay closer to the ear.
