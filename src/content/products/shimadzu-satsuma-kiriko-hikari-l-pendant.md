The appeal is the meeting of a simple outer shape and a busy field of light inside it. A long rectangular edge keeps the pendant graphic; the clear cuts interrupt the deeper blue-violet impression rather than making the whole surface one flat color.

Choose it when you want one focal point at the neckline. A plain knit leaves room for the geometry, while a large patterned scarf may compete with it. The official page is the place to compare both faces and the selected L variant; the illustration is an idea for styling.
