A pendant changes where a craft detail sits in an outfit. The Hosoge floral motif offers a central accent at the neckline, a different starting point from the small earrings that appear elsewhere in this selection.

We chose it as an ornamental alternative to a plain chain. Before deciding on the outfit, use the maker’s photographs and ask about the actual dimensions and cord or chain arrangement; those details are not supplied in the checked product text.

Yamato Urushi Kougei Sugimura states that it makes and exhibits work at Ganko Ittetsu Nagaya in Nara. The maker also lists a Lotus makie ring, offering another way to explore decoration worn on the body.
