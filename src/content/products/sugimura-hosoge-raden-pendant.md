Hosoge brings an entire ornamental floral design to the neckline. We chose it for the possibility of making a motif the central part of the outfit, rather than adding a tiny area of color or a small realistic flower.

Nakajima’s floral pendant offers a choice between two patterned faces. Here, the Hosoge motif itself is the starting point. Sugimura’s makie ring offers another useful comparison: whether you would prefer the decoration at your neckline or on your hand.
