We selected Hanabishi for showing a motif through its outline and the spaces within it. The warm copper color and open pattern work together, giving a different focus from a solid colored shape.

CHECOS offers a little silver flower, while ToyToy uses wood for an open pattern. The shared interest in motif can lead to different choices of metal color, material and scale. Copper lines are the reason to choose this one.
