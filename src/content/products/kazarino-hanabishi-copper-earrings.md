Hanabishi draws the motif with copper lines and open space. The color and the scale of that outline work together: it is not simply a flower picture reproduced in another material. Look at the complete silhouette as well as the central flower when comparing it with a compact metal blossom.

The unfilled areas distinguish it from ToyToy's incised wooden surface and from an enamel color plane. Copper is the material choice here, while the pierced construction is a fixed condition of this design. It is most useful on a shortlist when both the outline and that fastening suit what you are looking for.
