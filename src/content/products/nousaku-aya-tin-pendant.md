Aya gives a broad tin motif its own space on a long chain. Try it over a high neckline or the front of a simple dress, leaving other necklaces aside so the outline can be seen as one complete form.

The reason to choose it is the metal shape at the center of the clothing. Hamamatsuya puts wood pattern in that position, while RINZEN supplies a transparent green leaf. Aya is the candidate when you want the form itself, in a silver-colored palette, to lead the outfit.
