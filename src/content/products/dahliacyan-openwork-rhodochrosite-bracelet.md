This piece brings pattern to the wrist with small openings in the silver plate. At the centre is a round stone; at the sides the links are part of how it fits. A slightly different link position can change how much the plate moves while you wear it.

Compare the listed adjustable length with your wrist and with the edge of the cuff you wear most. For a softer wrist piece, the silk braid from Showen makes a useful material comparison.
