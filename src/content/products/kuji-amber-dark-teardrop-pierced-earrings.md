The attraction here is the contrast between the simple teardrop and the markings within it. Wear it when you want the amber itself to do the work, with a fine knit or the open collar of a shirt. The warm hue is quiet enough to sit alongside a watch or a plain metal ring.

For a gift, the origin is the part to point out. The maker also works with amber from other regions, and the individual listing is where it confirms that this piece uses amber mined in Kuji.
