A chain lets the carved flower sit centrally, while the same motif as a brooch can move to either side. Lay a similarly long chain over a knit or dress you already wear to find where this pendant could sit.

Both Hamamatsuya’s yosegi pendant and Yogado’s camellia feature wood. Choose the camellia when you want the eye to follow the carved petals. Choose the Hakone piece when you want repeat pattern from different wood tones.
