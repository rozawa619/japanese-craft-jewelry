The half-open flower is the detail to look for on the official photograph. Light across the carved edges makes the petals read separately even though they share a warm lacquer finish. On a wider lapel it can take the place of a printed detail.

Compare its rounded outline with the angular maple brooch. For a thin scarf, try the visual scale with a paper shape first and check how much fabric the pin will go through.
