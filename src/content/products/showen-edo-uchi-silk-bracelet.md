The small ball does more than finish the bracelet: it forms the visible meeting point of two wraps of braid. The design keeps a textile structure in view instead of disguising it as a metal chain. The closer color details within the blue are another reason to inspect the official photographs.

Choose it when a fine braided line is more appealing than a broad cuff surface. The ball-and-loop construction also belongs in that decision, particularly if you normally use an adjustable chain. Material, visible closure and the way the line wraps around the wrist work together here.
