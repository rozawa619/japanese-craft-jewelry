A pendant gives yosegi a different role from an earring: the wooden surface can be seen together, at the center of the clothing. The square wooden element and longer chain suggest wearing the pattern over a layer rather than treating it as a tiny neckline detail.

RINZEN’s pendant emphasizes a green leaf outline, while Nakajima’s offers two different faces. Here, the repeated colors of the woods are the reason to choose it. It is a pattern-led option for the person less interested in a recognizable floral motif.
