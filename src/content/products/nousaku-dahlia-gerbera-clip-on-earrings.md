A flower can supply detail without adding a strongly colored stone. In these tin ornaments, the petal arrangement is the part to compare: Dahlia and Gerbera give two outlines within the same silver-colored direction. Look at both official flower options rather than selecting only by the combined listing name.

The choice also differs from a long flower drop. Here the small motif gathers detail near the ear, leaving the petal form to do the decorating. Compare a flower's material and construction after choosing its outline; the tin ornament and its separate silver fittings are not one identical metal throughout.
