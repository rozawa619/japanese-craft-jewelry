The appeal of ori is the space taken up by folded cloth rather than a polished gemstone. Its spreading outline gives this pair a different role from CO.COON’s thread-worked Frame Series. Consider it when you want the texture of a textile to be visible around the face.

CO.COON brings together silk thread and cloth dyed and handwoven by Hakata-ori artist Kiyo Araki with the work of accessory makers. The official gallery credits this design to hanaroi and identifies it as non-pierced earrings. It is a catalog introduction: the currently offered colors, price and fitting details need confirmation with the brand.
