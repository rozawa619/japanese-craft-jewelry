Wooden jewelry can be a choice about geometry as well as natural color. In design D, the bar and chevron-like pattern run in the same direction, giving the composition its vertical emphasis.

C is more motif-led, while CHECOS brings a small flower in silver. D earns its place through the more abstract outline. It is a useful candidate when you want geometry without another print in the clothing.
