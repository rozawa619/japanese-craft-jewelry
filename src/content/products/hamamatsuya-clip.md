We selected this listing as a non-pierced way to explore the geometric wood colors of yosegi. Hamamatsuya lists it separately from its pierced options. The appeal is the craft detail; the fitting still needs to be checked with the maker.

### Pair it with

Try a plain shirt and soft cardigan for an understated look, or a simple rounded neckline for a gentler mood. These are styling suggestions, not a report of wearing this item. Choose the actual shape and pattern from the maker's current photographs.

### Find your fitting

Request a side or back view and ask whether the fastening uses a screw, spring or another mechanism. Confirm dimensions, weight and the material of each skin-contact part. We have not tested adjustment range, pressure or comfort.
