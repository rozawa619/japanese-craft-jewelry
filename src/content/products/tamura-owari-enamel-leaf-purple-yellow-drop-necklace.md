This pendant offers a few colors to play with, without filling the whole neckline. The pale areas leave breathing room between the purple and yellow curves. A cream shirt lets all three show; a darker top can make the yellow feel brighter. The detachable chain also gives you a way to try a chain you already own, after checking that it fits the bail.
