Gathered Kurume-kasuri fabric gives this design its flower-like volume, with a patterned bead below. We chose it for making the folds and visible cloth pattern the main ornament, rather than relying only on the outline of a flat motif.

Try the gathered form beside the straight lapel of a jacket, leaving room around the fabric. COYORIMI builds a rounded motif by knotting cord, while Morphosphere shows the outline of an embroidered-lace flower. EGAHO offers another textile choice through the way the cloth is gathered into shape.
