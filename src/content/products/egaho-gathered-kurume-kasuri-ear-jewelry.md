Gathering the cloth makes its folds part of the ornament. The kasuri pattern is seen across a three-dimensional fabric form rather than a flat panel, with a patterned bead below it. Choose this direction when the way cloth takes shape is more appealing than a precisely repeated flat outline.

COYORIMI makes a round detail by knotting cord; Morphosphere emphasizes a lace flower. EGAHO offers another textile structure, and its fitting options provide a separate practical decision. Select the fastening you want before treating the available cloth colors as equivalent versions for your use.
