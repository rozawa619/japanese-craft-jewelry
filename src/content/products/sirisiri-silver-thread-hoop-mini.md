Metallic-thread loops give this ornament both a visible outline and open spaces. SIRI SIRI uses Kyoto-made silver thread shaped by rattan craftspeople, bringing the detail of a thread construction into a broad ear-jewelry form.

Try the loops beside the clean shape of a structured coat, leaving a busy scarf away from the same area. Page Blue from the same brand makes an enamel surface the focal point; here, the lines and the gaps between them lead the composition. Choose it when an open outline appeals more than a solid hoop.
