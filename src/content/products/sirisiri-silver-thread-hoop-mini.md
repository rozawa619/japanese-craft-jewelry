A broad hoop need not be a filled metal surface. Here the silver thread builds a lace-like outline with spaces between the loops. The fine wrapped lines give something different to examine from the smooth body of a conventional hoop.

The maker connects Kyoto-made thread with the work of rattan craftspeople, without listing rattan as the material of this item. That distinction is part of why we included it: a craft technique can create a new material expression. Choose it for the thread construction and its changing appearance, after checking the actual size rather than the mini name alone.
