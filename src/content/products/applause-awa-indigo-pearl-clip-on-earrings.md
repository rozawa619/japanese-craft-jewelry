A pearl does not have to stay white to be the quiet part of an outfit. Here, blue gathers in the uneven surface and rounded outline, while the short silver-tone chain keeps a little space between the lobe and the drop.

The choice is about color and shape together. Beside a plain white collar, the blue can be a small accent; beside a strongly patterned scarf it may recede. Compare the actual seller photographs for each irregular pearl rather than expecting two perfectly matching manufactured spheres.
