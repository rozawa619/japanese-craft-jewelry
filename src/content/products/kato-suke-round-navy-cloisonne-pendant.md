The outline is quiet: one circle, a narrow rim, a fine chain. Inside that circle, the blue changes from a deeper edge to brighter flecks as the light shifts. It gives a plain collar a small, concentrated point of color. This is an enamel pendant, so its appeal comes from a fired surface over metal, rather than a cut stone.
