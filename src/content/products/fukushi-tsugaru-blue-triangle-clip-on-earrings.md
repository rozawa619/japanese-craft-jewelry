There is a practical reason to choose this long shape. The flat face leaves enough room for the blue surface to register even with dark hair, and the sharp outline gives plain clothing a stronger accent than a tiny stud. The two-sided finish matters because a hanging piece turns as you move.

The workshop gives the length and the width of the base, so you can compare the silhouette before ordering. Check the photographs on the maker’s page and ask about the fittings if you have a preferred clip style.
