Choose the stud when you want the shape to stay close to the ear. Its rounded outline makes a useful contrast to stripes, collars and the straight lines of a jacket; the peacock option also offers a different mood from familiar white pearl jewelry.

Compared with the swing version in our Osaka selection, this is the shorter, more contained silhouette. Both fitting types share one official listing, so choose the design first, then check the color and fitting together.

The regional connection is the shell-pearl component, identified by the retailer as Senshu, Osaka. The listing says made in Japan, but it does not identify the individual workshop. These are imitation pearls, not cultured pearls.
