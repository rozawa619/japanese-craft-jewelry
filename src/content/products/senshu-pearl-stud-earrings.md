We selected the studs for a meaningful color choice within a simple form. White and peacock let you decide between brightness and depth without changing the basic round outline.

The Senshu swing version suspends the bead below the ear. This one keeps the circle compact. Comparing the two by length and placement is more useful than treating every round pearl-style earring as the same choice.
