A pearl-like bead is familiar; a clear square beside it changes how the whole shape reads. This Basic1 selection brings cotton pearl together with the cellulose acetate used for eyeglass frames. Instead of adding a second bright color, the clear version leaves light and the edge of the form to do the work.

It is an interesting starting point for someone who likes pale jewelry but wants more than a solitary round stud. Pair it with a white blouse or cool gray knit, then compare with an Akoya stud to decide whether the material of the pearl or the outline around it matters more to you.
