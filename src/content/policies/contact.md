For an editorial correction, include the ITO page URL, the detail that needs attention and a primary source that supports the correction. For questions about image rights or a product's attribution, identify the specific item or illustration.

Use the contact method displayed on this page. If it opens a public issue tracker, keep your message limited to information you are comfortable publishing. Do not post private addresses, payment information or retailer order documents.

## Questions about an order

Please contact the retailer directly about product availability, materials, payment, shipping, damage, cancellation or returns. ITO is an editorial site and cannot access or change a retailer's orders.

## Makers and retailers

If you would like us to consider a piece of jewelry, provide its official product page, the maker and production location, published specifications and clear image-use terms. Sending a suggestion does not guarantee inclusion or create an affiliate relationship.
