Japanese Craft Jewelry does not use its own analytics, advertising trackers or cookies. There are no user accounts or input forms.

## Website hosting

The hosting service may record technical information needed to deliver pages, including an IP address and request time. See [GitHub Pages and data collection](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection).

## External links

Other websites have their own privacy policies. When an affiliate link is used, the destination or its affiliate service may use technology to identify the referring site.

We will update this page if the way this site handles information changes.
