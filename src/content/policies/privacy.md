ITO is a static editorial website. It does not provide user accounts, a shopping cart, payment processing or an on-site contact form. We do not use analytics scripts, advertising trackers or cookies set by this site's own application.

## Hosting and technical requests

Your browser requests pages and assets from the service hosting the website. That service may process technical request information, such as an IP address, request time and browser information, under its own policies. This page does not promise that the hosting service keeps no logs.

## External retailers and source websites

Following an external link takes you to another service with its own privacy practices. If a link is identified as an affiliate link, the retailer or its network may use attribution technology under its terms. Read the relevant site's privacy and cookie information before continuing there.

ITO does not receive your retailer account credentials, payment information or parcel details through an on-site checkout, because we do not operate one.

## Contact and corrections

Use only the contact method displayed on our contact page. A third-party contact service may require an account and will apply its own privacy policy. If you post a public correction, its contents can be visible to other people. Do not include payment details, private order records, your home address or other sensitive information in a public report.

## Changes

If we introduce a new contact tool, analytics or other data collection, this policy must be updated to describe the actual implementation and any relevant choices before it is used. The date above indicates the latest revision of this text.
