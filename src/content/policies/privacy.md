Japanese Craft Jewelry does not use its own analytics, advertising trackers or cookies. There are no user accounts or input forms.

## Website hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed into GitHub. See [GitHub Pages and data collection](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection).

## External links

Selecting an official-site or Google Maps link opens an external website. This site does not collect your current location. External websites have their own privacy policies and may use cookies or identify the referring site. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en) for Google Maps.

## Maker and shop information

We use officially published professional and shop information where it is needed to introduce the jewelry. We do not collect private-life information unrelated to that purpose.

## Before adding advertising or analytics

Before introducing advertising tags or analytics, we will review the information sent, its purposes and recipients, and any required consent and user controls. We will update this page before that change takes effect.
