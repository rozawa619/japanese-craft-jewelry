This policy explains browsing information and advertising at Japanese Craft Jewelry, operated by SORA.

**Google AdSense is not currently running on this site.** We do not currently use our own analytics or advertising trackers or set our own cookies. We do not offer accounts or forms for submitting personal information. The Google advertising sections below describe what will apply if advertising is introduced.

## Website hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed into GitHub. See [GitHub Pages and data collection](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection) and the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## External links

Selecting an official-site, retailer or external Google Maps link opens another website. External sites may receive your IP address and referring-page information and may use cookies under their own policies.

<h2 id="map-privacy">Displaying a map on this page</h2>

Choosing “Allow maps for 30 days” in a store section loads Google Maps. Your IP address and browser information are sent to Google, which may use cookies or similar technologies. Google processes this data to deliver maps and under its own privacy policy. No map connection is made while your choice is unset, off or expired. We do not collect your current location.

The Google entities handling information include Google LLC and, depending on your region and the processing involved, Google Ireland Limited. Google’s privacy policy explains their respective roles.

Your choice applies to all store maps on this site in both languages. We store only the on/off choice, policy version, save time and expiry in this device’s localStorage for 30 days; these are not sent to our server. While on, maps display automatically on later product pages. The 30-day period is our choice. An expired or outdated record is removed when next read and a fresh choice is needed. If browser storage is unavailable, your choice applies only on the current page.

Choose “Turn automatic maps off” below a map, or use “Google map settings” in any page footer, to stop automatic maps for all stores. This also updates other open tabs on the same site. Turning maps off cannot erase information already sent to Google or cookies set by Google. You can delete cookies in your browser settings. See [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en).

Jewelry, addresses and official store guides remain available with maps off. Choosing an external Google Maps link opens Google’s website. Map settings are separate from Google advertising consent and do not activate ads. Change your choice in the [map display settings](#map-preferences) below.

## If Google advertising is introduced

Google and other advertising providers may use cookies or similar identifiers to show ads based on previous visits to this site and other websites. Google advertising cookies can help Google and its partners show ads relevant to a visitor's interests.

Using Google's advertising services may send information such as the page URL and your IP address to Google. This information may be used to deliver ads, measure their effectiveness, prevent fraud and, depending on your settings and consent, personalize ads. See [how Google uses information from partner sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites?hl=en) and [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy?hl=en) for Google's processing and retention information.

Non-personalized advertising does not necessarily mean no cookies: cookies may still be used for frequency limits and aggregate reporting. We will identify the advertising providers and their information in the privacy settings before introducing their services.

<h2 id="advertising-choices">Advertising choices</h2>

- You can manage Google's personalized ads on partner sites through [Google Partner Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/partnerads). The available choices depend on your account, browser and device. Turning personalization off does not remove all advertising.
- [WebChoices](https://optout.aboutads.info/) provides controls for participating third-party advertising companies. Its choices do not cover every provider or every device.
- Your browser lets you block or delete cookies. This can also remove a saved advertising choice, so you may need to choose again. Google settings and browser controls are separate from consent given on this site.

## Consent and changing your choice

If advertising is introduced, we will not serve Google ads until the required privacy disclosures and consent arrangements are in place. Where consent is required, we will provide the purposes and recipients and allow you to refuse, choose, or later withdraw consent. A privacy-settings link will let you reopen those choices after introduction.

For personalized Google ads to visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we will use a Google-certified consent management platform that integrates with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. See [Google's consent requirements](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/13554116?hl=en).

There is currently no advertising-consent banner and no advertising consent saved by this site. We will explain the actual controls and participating providers here when they are introduced.

## Maker and shop information

We use officially published professional and shop information where it is needed to introduce the jewelry. We do not collect private-life information unrelated to that purpose.

## Changes to this policy

Before introducing advertising or analytics, or changing our hosting, we will update this page to match the information processed, purposes, recipients and available controls. Advertising will remain off until the necessary settings and notices are ready.
