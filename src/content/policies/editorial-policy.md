A small piece can make an everyday outfit feel more like you. We introduce jewelry with a connection to Japanese craft.

## Design and the making behind it

We look for colors, shapes and materials we want to wear, from wood and glass to porcelain, metal and fabric. We share what caught our eye and an idea for matching the piece with everyday clothes.

## Details from official sources

We check makers and official shops, keep unanswered questions clear and update a page when we find new information.

## AI styling images

Styling images are created with AI and labeled “AI image.”

## Clear affiliate notices

If a link can earn a referral fee, we identify it as an affiliate link. The same care goes into our selection whether a link earns a fee or not.
